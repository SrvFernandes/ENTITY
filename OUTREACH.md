## 2. Standards Communities & Working Groups

Technical review and independent counterexample requests:

* **W3C Credentials Community Group (CCG)** — Verifiable credentials and data integrity test vectors.
* **W3C Web Identity & Credentials Adoption CG** — Interoperability and browser ecosystem integration.
* **Decentralized Identity Foundation (DIF)** — Identifier resolution and cryptographic commitments.
* **OpenID Foundation Digital Credentials WG** — Protocol conformance and authorization profiles.
* **Kantara Initiative** — Identity assurance and trust frameworks.
* **OpenSSF (Open Source Security Foundation)** — Supply-chain security and reproducible builds.
* **Research Software Directory** — Research software citation and reproducibility tracking.

> **Note:** Outreach submissions are strictly for technical peer review, formal counterexamples, and interoperability feedback, not commercial endorsements.
