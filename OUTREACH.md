## Bounty Program Guidelines

### Eligible Categories
1. **Clean-environment reproduction** on unsupported OS/architecture
2. **Documentation fixes** with merged corrective PR
3. **Independent test vectors** with counterexamples
4. **Package registry readiness** (from #71)
5. **External verifier implementations** against sealed interfaces

### Rules
- Bounties require **accepted PR/evidence** for payout
- Must be **impossible to satisfy** by modifying vectors/hashes
- Payment **does not** constitute independent validation
- No predetermined answers allowed

### Platforms
- [Algora](https://algora.io/)
- [IssueHunt](https://issuehunt.io/)
